Safety Tips

Stay safe when finding and hiring professionals through FixAm Lagos.

Your Safety Matters

FixAm Lagos is designed to help clients connect with service professionals. Always use good judgment and take reasonable safety precautions when arranging a service.

Check Professional Details

Review the professional's profile, experience, services and customer ratings before making a booking.

Communicate Clearly

Clearly explain the job, location, expected service and other important details before the professional arrives.

Confirm the Location

Make sure the service location and appointment details are correct before the job begins.

Discuss the Cost

Agree on the expected price, service requirements and any additional charges before work starts.

Protect Your Home

Keep valuable belongings secure and only provide access to areas necessary for the requested service.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you notice suspicious behavior or feel unsafe, stop the service and contact FixAm Lagos support.

Emergency Situation?

For immediate danger or a serious emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service in your area. Do not rely only on the FixAm Lagos platform for emergency response.
