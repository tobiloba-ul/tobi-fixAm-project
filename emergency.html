Emergency Services

Get important emergency contacts and guidance when you need help.

In an Emergency

If you are facing immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service directly. FixAm Lagos should not be used as a replacement for emergency response services.

Police

Contact the police when you need urgent assistance involving personal safety, security or a crime.

Call 112

Medical Emergency

Seek immediate medical assistance for serious illness, injury or other urgent health situations.

Call 112

Fire Emergency

Contact emergency services immediately if there is a fire or another serious fire-related danger.

Call 112

Road Emergency

For serious road incidents or accidents, contact emergency responders and move to a safe location if possible.

Call 112

Security Assistance

If you feel threatened or unsafe, move to a safe place and contact the appropriate emergency service.

Call 112

FixAm Support

For problems relating to your FixAm Lagos account, booking or platform experience, contact our support team.

Contact Support

Important Safety Reminder

Always provide emergency responders with your location and clear information about the situation. If possible, stay in a safe place while waiting for help.
