About FixAm Lagos

FixAm Lagos is a digital platform designed to connect residents with trusted and skilled professionals for their home and repair needs.

Who We Are

FixAm Lagos is a service marketplace created to make it easier for people in Lagos to find reliable professionals for different home and maintenance services.

Instead of spending time searching for a technician or artisan, users can explore professionals based on their service, location and ratings.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make finding trusted professionals simple, convenient and accessible to residents across Lagos.

We also help skilled professionals showcase their services and connect with customers who need their expertise.

Our Vision

To build a trusted digital community where residents can easily find skilled professionals and professionals can grow their businesses through meaningful connections with customers.
